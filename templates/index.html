WHAT IS WAR DIALING

War dialing is the practice of automatically scanning telephone numbers to find modems, computers, and other systems connected to phone lines. The technique became widespread in the early 1980s after being depicted in the 1983 film WarGames. Practitioners would dial hundreds of numbers overnight and log which ones answered with a modem carrier tone. What they found on the other end ranged from university systems to corporate PBX switches to government computers. This operation log records findings from active scans.
